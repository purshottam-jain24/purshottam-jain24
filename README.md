### About

I'm Purshottam Jain, though most people call me Piyush. I'm a full-stack software engineer in Delhi, India, and I take products from idea to production and keep them running. Lately that's meant an AI interview platform used by 7,000+ people, a multi-tenant email platform for businesses, and a feedback platform used by 8,000+ people.

I care about speed, clean architecture, and software that still works when it grows.

<br>

### What I do

- **Full-stack web apps:** a polished Next.js and React front end backed by real APIs, from a blank file to something people actually use.
- **AI-powered features:** AI that does real work, like voice interviews, multilingual transcription and generated content, running in production rather than as demos.
- **Backends and scalable APIs:** multi-tenant SaaS, REST and GraphQL services and real-time data, built with Node.js, Go and Fastify.
- **Cloud, infra and performance:** Docker, CI/CD and self-hosting, plus the tuning that keeps products fast under load.
